Community Guidelines

Amouraéx is built for respectful, intentional connections. By using the platform, you agree to follow these guidelines.

Respect & Behavior

  • Treat all users with respect
  • No harassment, hate speech, threats, or sexual violence threats
  • No bullying or intimidation
  • No repeated unwanted contact, coercion, stalking, or behavior meant to corner, shame, or pressure another user

Authenticity

  • No fake profiles or impersonation
  • Provide accurate information
  • Do not mislead other users
  • Do not submit false identity, age, profile, or verification information
  • Your primary profile photo should clearly represent you
  • Blank images, walls, covered-camera shots, heavily obscured photos, non-human primary images, or other misleading profile pictures may be removed or sent for review

Age & Eligibility

Amouraéx is for adults 18+ only. You may not use the service if you are under 18, claim to be under 18, help a minor use the platform, or misrepresent age to access dating or social features.

Underage claims in a profile, message, Blind Date exchange, or other app surface may trigger an automatic age review, temporary account lock, discovery removal, verification request, or account removal.

Safety

  • No exploitation or manipulation
  • No sexual coercion, threats of sexual assault, or pressure to engage in sexual activity
  • No solicitation or scams
  • No sharing of harmful or illegal content
  • No attempts to bypass moderation, verification, age review, discoverability limits, or other safety controls
Violations may result in permanent account removal.

Child Safety and CSAE

Amouraéx and Æffinityone LLC explicitly prohibit Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE), Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM), grooming, sextortion, sexualization of minors, and any attempt to exploit, abuse, or endanger children. Amouraéx is for adults 18+ only, and minors are not permitted to use the service.

Suspected child safety violations should be reported in-app or by contacting admin@aeffinityone.com. More details are available in our Child Safety Standards.

Content Rules

  • No explicit or illegal content
  • No promotion of harmful behavior
  • No unauthorized advertising
  • No scams, paid sexual solicitation, financial manipulation, or attempts to move users into unsafe off-platform transactions
  • No sexually violent, coercive, non-consensual, or threatening content
  • No misuse of photos, chat photos, voice notes, moments, reports, verification materials, boosts, spotlights, or activity features

Enforcement

We reserve the right to remove content, restrict features, request verification, limit discoverability, force-log out accounts, place accounts into age review, preserve evidence, or permanently ban accounts that violate these guidelines. Severe threats, sexual violence threats, child safety violations, or repeated abusive behavior may receive immediate enforcement attention.
