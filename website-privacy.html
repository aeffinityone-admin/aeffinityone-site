Privacy for the Æffinityone company website.

This policy explains how Æffinityone LLC collects, uses, stores, and handles information submitted through the company website, including service inquiry and contact forms.

Æffinityone LLC Website Privacy

Company website data collection, service inquiry handling, and contact information use.

Information we collect

We may collect information you provide directly through this website, including your name, company or brand name, email address, project type, platform needs, ownership preferences, budget model, budget range, timeline, project summary, and business goals.

How we use it

We use inquiry information to review project fit, respond to service requests, understand potential partnership opportunities, evaluate business needs, and manage follow-up communication.

Form processing

Website inquiry submissions may be processed through third-party form infrastructure. At the time of this policy, Æffinityone uses Formspree to receive and route website form submissions.

Form processors may store submissions and deliver notifications according to their own infrastructure and policies.

What we do not promise

Submitting an inquiry does not guarantee project acceptance, a proposal, a partnership offer, or a client relationship. Inquiry data may be reviewed internally to determine next steps.

Retention

We retain website inquiry and contact information for as long as reasonably necessary to evaluate inquiries, respond to communications, maintain business records, protect against abuse, and support legitimate operational needs.

Sharing

We do not sell website inquiry information. We may share information with service providers that support form handling, communications, hosting, analytics, security, or business operations, subject to their service roles.

Your choices

You may contact us to request correction or deletion of eligible inquiry information, subject to legal, security, recordkeeping, and operational limitations.

Related product policies

This website privacy policy applies to the company website and service inquiries. Product and app data practices are governed by the separate app-specific privacy policies linked in the Legal Center.

Contact

For website privacy questions, contact admin@aeffinityone.com.
