Amouraéx Child Safety Standards

Amouraéx is committed to maintaining a safe environment for all users. Our platform is strictly intended for individuals aged 18 and older. We enforce a zero-tolerance policy toward child sexual abuse, exploitation, or any form of inappropriate conduct involving minors.

Age Restrictions

All users must be at least 18 years old to use Amouraéx. Accounts found to be in violation will be permanently banned.

Prevention Measures

  • Strict 18+ age requirement
  • User reporting and blocking features
  • Manual review of flagged accounts
  • Keyword-based moderation systems

Reporting

Users can report suspicious or inappropriate behavior directly within the app. Reports are reviewed promptly by our moderation team.

Enforcement

Any content or behavior involving minors will result in immediate account termination and may be reported to appropriate law enforcement authorities.
