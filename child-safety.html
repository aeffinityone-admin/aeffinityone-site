Child Safety

Child Safety Standards

Amouraéx is committed to maintaining a safe environment for all users. The platform is strictly intended for individuals aged 18 and older.

Age Restrictions

All users must be at least 18 years old. Accounts found to be in violation will be permanently banned.

Prevention Measures

  • Strict 18+ enforcement
  • User reporting and blocking tools
  • Manual moderation review
  • Keyword-based detection systems

Reporting

Users can report suspicious or inappropriate behavior directly within the app.

Enforcement

Any content or behavior involving minors results in immediate account termination and may be reported to law enforcement.
