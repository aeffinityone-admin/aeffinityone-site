Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Æffinityone LLC collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects information when you use Qonstellation, a private book creation and visual storytelling app.

Who We Are

Qonstellation is operated by Æffinityone LLC. For privacy, account, or data requests, contact dri@qonstellations.app.

Information We Collect

Account Information

Email address, account identifier, sign-in provider, verification status, subscription tier, and account preferences. Google or Apple may provide limited sign-in information when you choose those methods.

Book Content

Book titles, author names, covers, story text, page order, bookmarks, drafts, uploaded images, editing metadata, and recently deleted book records that you choose to create or save.

Purchases & Entitlements

Product identifiers, purchase status, transaction references, entitlement status, and renewal information needed to provide subscription features. Apple and Google process payment credentials; Qonstellation does not receive your complete card number.

Device & Service Data

App version, device platform, notification permission and token, authentication events, service errors, crash information, security signals, and diagnostics needed to operate and protect the app.

Support Information

Messages, contact details, screenshots, and other information you provide when requesting account, billing, privacy, or technical support.

Photo Access

Qonstellation accesses photos only when you choose images for a cover or story page. Selected images may be uploaded to cloud storage so they remain available with your book.

How We Use Information

Provide the Studio

Authenticate your account, save and sync books, display your library, restore eligible deleted books, and provide editing and preview features.

Subscriptions

Apply plan limits, verify purchases, restore entitlements, and provide customer support for subscription access.

Safety & Reliability

Prevent abuse, protect account access, diagnose crashes, monitor service health, enforce product limits, and improve app performance.

Communications

Send authentication, verification, password-reset, service, support, and optional notification messages related to your account and books.

Storage, Sharing, and Retention

Service Providers

Qonstellation uses service providers such as Firebase and the Apple and Google platforms for authentication, database storage, file storage, notifications, diagnostics, and purchases. They process information under their own terms and privacy commitments.

No Book Distribution

Qonstellation is a private creation tool, not a public book marketplace or social feed. We do not publish your books for other users to browse unless a future sharing feature is clearly presented and you choose to use it.

Recently Deleted Books

Books moved to Recently Deleted may remain recoverable for up to 30 days before permanent deletion. Related uploaded files may be removed during permanent deletion and cleanup.

Account Deletion

In-app account deletion removes eligible account data, books, story pages, covers, images, settings, and authentication access. Limited records may be retained when required for legal compliance, billing, security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or enforcement.

Legal Disclosures

We may disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect users, Æffinityone, the service, or the rights and safety of others.

Business Changes

Information may be transferred as part of a merger, financing, reorganization, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to applicable law and appropriate safeguards.

Your Choices and Rights

You may update account settings, control notifications through the app and device settings, delete individual books, recover eligible books during the recovery window, or delete your account in Settings. You may also contact us to request access, correction, deletion, or a copy of eligible personal information, subject to applicable law and identity verification.

Children, Security, and Changes

Qonstellation is not directed to children under 13. We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards, but no storage or transmission system can be guaranteed completely secure. We may update this policy as the app or law changes and will update the date above when we do.
