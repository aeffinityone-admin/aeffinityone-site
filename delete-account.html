Delete Your Account

If you would like to delete your account and associated data, follow the steps below.

In-App Deletion

  1. Open the Amouraéx app
  2. Go to Settings
  3. Select Account
  4. Tap Delete Account

Email Request

Contact us at:

admin@aeffinityone.com

What Happens After Deletion

  • Your profile and data are removed
  • Your photos are deleted
  • Your conversations are removed
Some data may be temporarily retained for legal, security, or fraud prevention purposes.
