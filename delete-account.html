Delete Your Amouraéx Account

If you would like to delete your account and associated data, follow the steps below:

In-App Deletion

  1. Open the Amouraéx app
  2. Go to Settings
  3. Select Account
  4. Tap Delete Account

Request via Email

You can also request account deletion by contacting us:

Email: admin@aeffinityone.com

What Happens When You Delete Your Account

  • Your profile and personal information will be deleted
  • Your photos will be removed
  • Your messages and conversations will be deleted

Some limited data may be retained for a short period where required for legal, security, or fraud prevention purposes.
