Safety & Reporting

Your safety is our priority. Amouraéx includes tools to help protect users and maintain a secure environment.

Reporting Users

  • Report inappropriate behavior directly in the app
  • Reports are reviewed by moderation
  • Evidence may be stored for safety review

Blocking

  • You can block any user at any time
  • Blocked users cannot contact you

Moderation

  • Content may be reviewed for safety
  • Accounts may be restricted or removed
Severe violations may result in permanent bans and law enforcement reporting.

Safety Tips

  • Do not share personal or financial information
  • Meet in public places
  • Trust your instincts
