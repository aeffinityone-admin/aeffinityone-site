How to delete your account

In Taskynx

Open Profile, scroll to Delete Account, review what will happen, and choose Schedule deletion. Company access is removed immediately and eligible account data is scheduled for permanent deletion after 30 days.

Support Request

Email admin@aeffinityone.com from the address associated with your account using the subject “Taskynx Account Deletion Request.” We may verify account ownership.

Team Member Accounts

Scheduling deletion removes company access immediately. Workplace records already submitted may remain as company operational records where retention is appropriate.

Company Owners

A primary company owner must contact Taskynx support to arrange an ownership transfer before deleting the personal account. Account deletion cannot close the company workspace or disable the team.

Data and subscriptions

Recovery Period

You may restore the account by signing in during the 30-day recovery period. After that period, eligible profile data, authentication access, personal settings, notification tokens, and other account-linked information are permanently deleted or de-identified.

Shared Company Records

Messages, assignments, proof, schedules, escalations, and audits remain with an active company workspace for operational continuity, review, disputes, security, and legitimate company recordkeeping. These records may continue to show the former user's name, user identifier, submitted text, timestamps, and evidence, but they no longer provide that user with account access.

Safety and Moderation Records

Safety evidence follows the retention periods described in the Taskynx Privacy Policy. Dismissed reports are generally scheduled for deletion after 30 days and actioned reports after 365 days, unless an open review, legal hold, dispute, security need, or legal obligation requires longer retention.

Store Subscription

Deleting Taskynx does not automatically cancel an Apple App Store or Google Play subscription. The store account holder must manage or cancel the subscription separately.

After 30 Days

Once permanent deletion is completed, the account cannot be recovered. A returning user will need a new invitation and account.
