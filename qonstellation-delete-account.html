You can permanently delete your Qonstellation account and eligible associated book data inside the app or request help from support.

Manage deletion from the app's Settings screen.

How To Delete Your Account

In the App

Open Qonstellation, go to Settings > Delete Account, review the warning, type DELETE, and select Delete My Account.

By Email

Email dri@qonstellations.app from the address connected to your account with the subject "Qonstellation Account Deletion Request."

Verification

We may ask for information needed to confirm account ownership before processing a support-assisted deletion request.

Permanent Action

Account deletion is permanent. Once processing completes, the account cannot be restored through the app.

What Happens to Your Data

Account Data

Eligible sign-in records, account profile data, settings, notification tokens, and Qonstellation account access are deleted or de-identified.

Book Data

Books, story pages, bookmarks, covers, uploaded images, drafts, and associated book metadata are removed as part of account deletion.

Limited Retention

Some records may be retained or anonymized when required or permitted for legal compliance, billing, security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or service enforcement.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not automatically cancel an Apple App Store or Google Play subscription. Cancel it separately through the store account used to subscribe.

Deleting One Book Instead

To keep your account but remove a book, press and hold the book in your library and choose Delete. Deleted books may remain recoverable in Settings > Recently Deleted for up to 30 days.
