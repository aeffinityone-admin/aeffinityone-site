Overview

What Makes Amouraéx Different

Amouraéx is a modern social connection platform that goes beyond profiles. Instead of endless swiping, users connect through real-time moments, meaningful interactions, and expressive identity. It combines social content, discovery, and messaging into one seamless experience designed to feel natural and real.

Features

Core Features

  • Moments: Share real-time photos that disappear after 24 hours
  • Profile discovery across local and global users
  • Messaging with text, voice, and media
  • Profile verification system
  • Built-in safety tools (report, block, moderation)
  • Privacy controls for visibility and communication
Privacy

Privacy & Control

Users have control over their experience, including who can view their profile, read receipts, and online visibility.
