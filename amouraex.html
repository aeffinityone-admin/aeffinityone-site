Overview

Share your world. Discover theirs.

Amouraéx is a modern social connection platform that goes beyond static profiles and endless swiping. It gives people more ways to be seen, more ways to discover others, and more ways to begin conversations with context instead of pressure.

Features

Core experiences inside Amouraéx

Moments

Share real-time photos that stay visible for up to 30 days and bring daily life into discovery.

Profile Discovery

Explore local and global users based on plan access, preferences, and discovery settings.

Messaging

Communicate through text, voice, and media in a flow designed to feel expressive and immediate.

Guided Experiences

Use Gaming Date, Blind Date, Date Wheel, and compatibility-led experiences to start differently.

Additional Platform Features

  • Compatibility Quiz experiences designed around relationship goals and personality fit
  • Profile verification system
  • Spotlight boosts and recent profile viewer features on higher tiers
  • Built-in safety tools including report, block, and moderation
  • Privacy controls for visibility and communication
