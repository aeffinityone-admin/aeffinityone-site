Name, email address, sign-in provider, account ID, display name, profile image, role, bio, and profile fields you choose to save.

Workspace names, cover art, descriptions, story descriptions, characters, ages, biographies, personalities, roles, powers, scenes, notes, ideas, invite codes, team roles, visual references, audio files, voice notes, and related metadata. AEXSpace may request camera, photo library, file, and microphone access so you can upload or create profile pictures, workspace covers, character references, scene images, audio references, and voice notes.

Subscriptions & Purchases

Plan level, product IDs, purchase tokens or transaction IDs, verification status, store source, renewal state, entitlement timing, and related purchase status. AEXSpace does not receive your full payment card number from Apple or Google.

Free access may include ads. Advertising services may process advertising identifiers, device identifiers, ad interactions, and related usage data to show, limit, measure, or improve ads where permitted by your settings and law.
